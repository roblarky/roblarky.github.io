StrokesPlus is now Input.Observer

Input.Observer is my new Windows app for gestures, hotkeys and text expansion: draw a shape, press a key or type a short code, and it runs the action you set for that app. It is rebuilt from the ground up, and its engine makes no network connections.

It brings over your StrokesPlus.net setup. Gestures, hotkeys, app groups and settings come across; scripts are translated where possible, so a script-heavy setup needs some hand work.

If you donated to StrokesPlus or StrokesPlus.net (any amount), translated either app, made 5 or more posts across the two forums, signed in to StrokesPlus.net from the app, or wrote to me about StrokesPlus, your Input.Observer 1.x license is free.
